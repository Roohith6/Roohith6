## 👨‍💻 About Me

Hi, I'm **Roohith R**, an **Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE)** undergraduate at **VIT Vellore (Class of 2027)** passionate about **RTL Design**, **Computer Architecture**, **FPGA Prototyping**, and **Digital IC Verification**.

- 🔭 **What I Do**: Architect and simulate digital hardware from microarchitecture specs down to physical synthesis.
- ⚡ **Specialties**: Building custom **RISC-V SoCs** with memory-mapped peripherals, accelerating **Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs)** on FPGAs for edge intelligence, and implementing real-time DSP pipelines.
- 🛠️ **Industry EDA Flows**: Hands-on experience with **Cadence (Genus, Xcelium, Innovus)**, **Intel Quartus Prime**, **ModelSim**, **Icarus Verilog**, **Yosys**, and open-source ASIC synthesis flows (**OpenROAD**).
- 🎯 **Current Focus**: Hardware Design Verification (DV) using SystemVerilog, UVM, and embedded firmware development with Zephyr RTOS.
